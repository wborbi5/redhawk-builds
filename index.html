Challenges

Thirty prompts. Six tracks.

Pick one problem. Starter pack v1.1 includes the full prompt and three suggested MVPs for each.

Track

Investment Banking and Commercial Banking

  1. 1

    Deal due diligence. Find gaps and inconsistencies in company documents; link findings to evidence.

  2. 2

    Comparable-company analysis. Select peers and assemble financial metrics with traceable sources.

  3. 3

    Business financing comparison. Compare loan costs, repayment schedules and restrictions.

  4. 4

    Suspicious transaction review. Prioritize alerts and investigate related activity using synthetic data.

  5. 5

    Borrower covenant monitoring. Check borrower reports against loan requirements and flag exceptions.

Track

Accounting, Audit and Financial Controls

  1. 6

    Audit evidence organizer. Match documents to requests and identify missing support.

  2. 7

    Financial record reconciliation. Match transactions, invoices, receipts and ledger entries for review.

  3. 8

    Financial variance analysis. Explain differences across actuals, budgets and prior periods.

  4. 9

    Month-end close coordination. Track dependencies, unresolved balances and reviewer approvals.

  5. 10

    Purchasing and expense controls. Flag duplicate payments, missing approvals and policy exceptions.

Track

Consulting, Business Operations and Financial Planning

  1. 11

    Vendor and software spending. Find duplicate tools, unused subscriptions and renewal opportunities.

  2. 12

    Business process improvement. Identify bottlenecks and estimate benefits from workflow changes.

  3. 13

    Business cash flow forecasting. Anticipate cash shortages and test payment-timing scenarios.

  4. 14

    Invoice collections. Track overdue invoices, prioritize follow-up and draft reminders.

  5. 15

    Startup runway planning. Model how hiring, spending, revenue and fundraising affect cash.

Track

Investment Research, Wealth Management and Education

  1. 16

    Company filing analysis. Compare reporting periods and changed disclosures with source evidence.

  2. 17

    Portfolio concentration. Reveal overlapping holdings and company or sector exposure.

  3. 18

    Investment fee impact. Model long-term fee costs with editable assumptions.

  4. 19

    Financial claim checks. Verify headlines, memos and presentations against source documents.

  5. 20

    3D investing game. Teach risk, diversification, liquidity and fees through guided missions.

Track

Student and Campus Finance

  1. 21

    Variable-income budgeting. Plan around changing income, upcoming bills and potential shortfalls.

  2. 22

    Fair shared expenses. Manage unequal contributions, changing participation and balances.

  3. 23

    Full-cost housing comparison. Compare rent, deposits, utilities, transportation and lease length.

  4. 24

    Student organization finances. Track budgets, reimbursements, event spending and officer handoffs.

  5. 25

    First job offer comparison. Compare compensation, benefits, relocation and living costs.

Track

Blockchain and Crypto

  1. 26

    Stablecoin invoice-to-cash. Match USDC payments to invoices and export AR aging.

  2. 27

    Crypto cost-basis ledger. Reconcile exchange and wallet activity; compute FIFO versus HIFO gains.

  3. 28

    Stablecoin treasury risk monitor. Track peg deviation, concentration and rebalance alerts.

  4. 29

    Counterparty wallet screening. Triage wallet risk into approve, review or reject with a logged rationale.

  5. 30

    AI agent spend controls. Bound agent USDC payments with policy, escalation and an audit trail.

Participant resources

Redhawk starter pack · v1.1

Includes 30 challenge prompts, 90 MVP ideas, financial sample data, a Supabase backend foundation, source evidence tools, and setup guides.

Download starter pack ZIP

Extract the ZIP and read START-HERE.txt. You can also upload it to an assistant that supports ZIP files. If yours does not, upload the included LLM-STARTER-PACK.txt.

Local examples work without API keys. Cloud services require your own setup. This pack provides backend code and data; teams build their interface and choose any AI integrations.

Judging

100 points. Six categories.

The same rubric applies across all tracks and both judging rounds.

Redhawk Builds judging rubric, totaling 100 points
CategoryPointsWhat judges look for
Problem and business relevance20A clear user and a meaningful financial or business problem.
Working solution25A functioning core workflow and a demonstration of what the team built.
Accuracy and trustworthiness20Correct calculations, traceable sources, and clear assumptions and limitations.
Usefulness and usability15A practical, understandable workflow that helps the intended user.
Originality and thoughtful execution10Meaningful decisions and improvements beyond the starting materials.
Pitch and demonstration10A clear explanation of the problem, a focused demo, evidence of value, and direct answers to questions.
Total100Score each category from 1 to 5, then multiply its points by the score divided by 5.

Scoring: 1 = largely missing; 2 = limited; 3 = solid; 4 = strong; 5 = exceptional. Pitch points reward clarity and evidence, not expensive slides or presentation polish.

Table judging

Sunday, 10:30–11:30 AM. Aim for a pitch under two minutes. Each team is scheduled for three independent judge scores; average weighted scores select five finalists.

Finals

Sunday at 1 PM. Each finalist gets a four-minute pitch and three minutes of Q&A. Fresh final-round scores determine the top three.

Best Use of ElevenLabs

Opt in through your Devpost submission and show the integration. Judges vote on useful purpose, reliability, and improved experience. Any eligible team can win, including an overall winner.

Ties: compare working-solution scores, then accuracy and trustworthiness, then panel discussion. Starter code is welcome: judges evaluate your team's additions and decisions. Complexity alone earns no extra points.

FAQ

Before you register.

Do I need to know how to code?

No. Teams need finance minds, designers, and builders.

What does it cost?

Nothing. Redhawk Builds is free.

Do I need a team?

Yes. Teams of 3 to 5 are required.

What do we submit?

A working demo, a repository, a project description, your team, and a video of two minutes or less. Say which data and integrations are real and which are mocked. The repository must be created or made available after Saturday, Nov 7 at 10:00 AM Eastern. Devpost submissions are due Sunday, Nov 8 at 10:00 AM Eastern.

What should I bring?

A laptop, a charger, and your student ID. Bring hardware if you want to use it.

Is the building open all night?

No. Friday ends at 8 PM. Saturday the venue closes at 10 PM and opens again Sunday at 8:30 AM.
