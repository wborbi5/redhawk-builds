FAQ

Before you register.

Do I need to know how to code?

No. Teams need finance minds, designers, and builders.

What does it cost?

Nothing. Redhawk Builds is free.

Do I need a team?

No. Teams are up to 4. Solo builders get matched at the Friday mixer.

What should I bring?

A laptop, a charger, and whatever you need to stay overnight.

Is it overnight?

Yes. Building is open through the weekend.

How do I submit?

On Devpost, by 10 AM Sunday.
