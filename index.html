BEFORE YOU REGISTER

Good
questions.

Who can attend?

Redhawk Builds is for Miami University students. First-time builders are welcome, including business, data science, and CS students. We’re planning for approximately 100 attendees.

How big are teams?

Teams have 3–5 people. Bring a team or come ready to meet other students.

Can we use AI?

Yes. AI is allowed with disclosure. Explain what you used and how it contributed to your work.

Where do I register and submit?

Register through Typeform, the registration source of truth. Luma is for discovery and RSVP; complete Typeform to register. Submit your project on Devpost.

Where will event updates happen?

GroupMe is the event operations chat. Check it for logistics and updates.
