Details

Good to know.

Mentors

Office hours all Saturday, 10 AM to 10 PM. Two or three mentors on at a time, one help queue.

Food

Six meals are free, plus snacks, coffee, and water. Dietary options are labeled. Ask for what you need on the registration form.

Judging

Every team demos at its table Sunday morning, to about three judges. The top 5 pitch in finals: 4 minutes, then 3 minutes of questions. Fresh scores pick 1st, 2nd, and 3rd. There is also an audience vote award.

Teams

Up to 4. Solo is fine. Mixer Saturday at 10 if you do not have a team yet.

What to bring

Laptop, charger, student ID, and any hardware you want to use.

Build hours

Building starts Saturday at 10 AM. The venue closes at 10 PM Saturday and reopens at 8:30 AM Sunday.

FAQ

Before you register.

Do I need to know how to code?

No. Teams need finance minds, designers, and builders.

What does it cost?

Nothing. Redhawk Builds is free.

Do I need a team?

No. Teams are up to 4, and solo is fine. There is a mixer Saturday at 10 if you want one.

What should I bring?

A laptop, a charger, and your student ID. Bring hardware if you want to use it.

Is the building open all night?

No. Friday ends at 8 PM. Saturday the venue closes at 10 PM and opens again Sunday at 8:30 AM.
